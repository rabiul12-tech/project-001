Quick Tips & Tricks

Regular maintenance can prevent costly repairs. Check for leaks, test smoke detectors monthly, and clean gutters seasonally. For electrical issues, always turn off power at the breaker before inspection.

Know where your main water shut-off valve is located to minimize damage in case of a pipe burst. For minor drywall repairs, keep a small patch kit handy.

Which services are eligible for warranty?

All our repair and installation services come with a 1-year warranty on labor. Parts and materials may have separate manufacturer warranties. Emergency services and consultations are not covered under warranty.

How quickly can you arrive?

For emergency services, we typically arrive within 2-4 hours. Standard appointments are scheduled within 24-48 hours. We offer same-day service for urgent repairs when available.

Do you offer same-day fixes?

Many common repairs can be completed the same day, including most plumbing fixes, electrical issues, and minor repairs. Larger projects like renovations may require multiple days based on scope.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the greater metropolitan area and surrounding suburbs. Service fees may apply for locations beyond 25 miles from our headquarters. Contact us with your zip code to confirm service availability.

How do quotes work?

We provide free, no-obligation quotes after assessing your project. Quotes are valid for 30 days and include all labor and materials. For larger projects, we may provide itemized estimates upon request.
